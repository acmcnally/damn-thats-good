Weeknight White Bean Chili

Serves 4 Dinner Freezer-friendly

Adapted from a Bon Appétit weeknight-dinners email, 2019.

Edit

Ingredients

For the chili
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 yellow onion, diced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 2 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp cayenne
  • 2 (15 oz) cans white beans, drained
  • 1 (4 oz) can diced green chiles
  • 3 cups chicken broth
For serving
  • Shredded cheddar
  • Sliced avocado
  • Lime wedges
  • Tortilla chips

Steps

For the chili
  1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
  2. Add onion and cook until soft, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in garlic, cumin, paprika, and cayenne; cook 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add turkey and cook, breaking it up, until browned.
  5. Stir in beans, chiles, and broth. Bring to a simmer.
  6. Reduce heat and simmer uncovered 25 minutes, stirring occasionally.
For serving
  1. Ladle into bowls and top with cheese, avocado, and a squeeze of lime.
